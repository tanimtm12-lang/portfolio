    Important Concepts

    What is a lattice?

    A lattice is an infinite periodic arrangement of points in space. Each lattice point has an identical environment.

    What is a unit cell?

    A unit cell is the smallest repeating geometrical region that can generate the entire crystal lattice by translation.

    What is a Bravais lattice?

    A Bravais lattice is a distinct periodic point arrangement consistent with translational symmetry in three dimensions.

    Why are there 14?

    The combination of crystal-system symmetry and allowed lattice-center translations produces exactly 14 unique three-dimensional Bravais lattices.

    Primitive lattice — P

    A primitive cell has lattice points only at its corners. The total contribution is one lattice point per primitive unit cell.

    Body-centered — I

    In addition to corner points, there is one lattice point at the center of the unit cell.

    Face-centered — F

    Lattice points occur at all corners and at the centers of all six faces. The effective number is four.

    Base-centered — C

    Additional lattice points occur at the centers of a pair of opposite faces.
